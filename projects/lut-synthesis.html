L-LUT to P-LUT Synthesis

Overview

FPGA fabric is built from small fixed-input LUTs (6-input on most modern devices), but some designs, particularly ones synthesised or generated programmatically, want to express much larger lookup tables. This project takes a large Boolean truth table (an "L-LUT") stored in hex format and decomposes it into a network of native 6-input LUTs (P-LUTs) that FPGA fabric can implement directly, optionally emitting a SystemVerilog netlist plus an exhaustive testbench to verify the decomposition is functionally identical to the original table.

Technical Approach

The toolflow implements five decomposition strategies of increasing sophistication:

  • Shannon Single: classic Shannon expansion with a fixed variable ordering.
  • Shannon Single Share: the same expansion, but caching and reusing matching LUT nodes to cut duplication.
  • Shannon Multi: multi-variable splits per level (4:1 mux stages) to reduce circuit depth.
  • Shannon Smart: two-variable splits chosen by an entropy and Hamming-distance heuristic, rather than a fixed order.
  • LDTC and CLUT: lossless differential table splitting and sub-table similarity analysis for further compression.

Each strategy is evaluated on LUT count, unique LUT instance count, and circuit depth, with Vivado-based post-synthesis area and timing evaluation planned.
